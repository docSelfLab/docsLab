Overview

QRSnap (the "App") is a fully free, no-in-app-purchase, no-ads, completely offline QR code and barcode scanner and generator. In this policy, "we" and the "Operator" mean the developer who publishes the App.

Our privacy principle is simple: we do not collect any personal data from you, and we do not track you. Scanning and generation run on your device. The Operator does not receive that information. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the App, including data that stays on your device and actions you choose to hand to iOS or another app.

1. Information We Collect

We collect no personal data whatsoever.

Specifically, the App does not collect or transmit any of the following:

  • Identity information such as name, email address, or phone number;
  • Precise or coarse location information;
  • Device identifiers (such as IDFA or any hardware serial number);
  • Usage data, analytics, or diagnostic logs;
  • Photos, contacts, calendar events, or other sensitive content.

With your permission, the App may read some of these categories on your device to complete an action you start (see Section 2). That information is not transmitted to the Operator, so it is not data we collect.

This is consistent with the App's privacy nutrition label submitted to the App Store: we declare No Data Collected and Tracking = No.

2. Permissions & Their Use

The App requests a system permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can decline or later revoke any permission in Settings. Declining a permission disables only that feature. Data read through these permissions stays on your device and is never uploaded to the Operator or any third party.

Camera

Used only for real-time scanning of QR codes and barcodes. The camera feed is processed on your device for recognition and is never recorded or uploaded.

Photo Library (read)

Used only when you explicitly select an image: either to recognize a QR code or barcode in that image, or to embed a logo while designing a code. The image is processed on your device and is not uploaded.

Photo Library (add)

Used only when you choose to save a generated or scanned code image to your photo library. The App writes that image through the system Photos API and does not upload it.

Contacts

Used only when you choose to fill a contact QR code from your address book, or to save a scanned contact card into Contacts. Contact details stay on your device and are not uploaded.

Calendar

Used only when you choose to add a scanned calendar event to Calendar. Event details stay on your device and are not uploaded.

Location (when in use)

Used only when you choose to fill coordinates from your current position while creating a location QR code. The App does not request background location, and coordinates are not uploaded.

Wi-Fi configuration

Used only when you choose to join a scanned Wi-Fi network. The App hands the network name and password to iOS so the system can connect. Those credentials are not uploaded to the Operator.

The App does not request microphone, health, or tracking permissions.

3. Local Data Storage

  • Records you scan and generate are saved in a database on your device (Apple SwiftData) to power the "History" feature;
  • A copy of a code you pin may be stored in an App Group on your device so the Home Screen Widget can display it. That copy is not synced to a server;
  • Appearance and scan-feedback preferences are stored in Apple UserDefaults on your device;
  • This data exists only on your device, is never synced to any cloud server, and is never read by the Operator;
  • You can delete individual entries or clear all history at any time within the App; uninstalling the App permanently removes this local data.

4. Network & Third Parties

  • The App does not connect to the Operator's servers and does not send your scans, generated codes, photos, contacts, calendar events, location, or Wi-Fi credentials to the Operator or to an analytics provider;
  • The App contains no advertising SDK, analytics/statistics SDK, crash-reporting SDK, or any other third-party library that collects data;
  • When you choose to open a link, map, email, phone number, or message, iOS hands that content to the browser or the relevant app. The App only provides a local safety heuristic for links (distinguishing HTTP/HTTPS and warning on suspicious links). It does not fetch or report the destination itself.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is rated 4+. It contains no ads, no data collection targeted at children, and no user-generated-content community. We do not knowingly collect personal data from users of any age.

6. Your Rights & Control

Because we collect no data from you, there is no need to request access, export, or deletion of data from us. All control over your local data is available within the App (see Section 3).

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update the version date at the top of this page. Material changes will be communicated via an in-app notice or a prominent notice on this page.

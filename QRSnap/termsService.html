2. Description of Service

The App is a QR code and barcode tool that primarily provides the following features:

  • Scan: recognize QR codes / barcodes in real time via camera, or from images in your photo library;
  • Create: generate QR codes / barcodes for URLs, Wi-Fi, contact cards, email, phone, text, location, calendar events, and more, with customizable styles and export;
  • History: store and manage your scanned and created records locally on your device;
  • Widget: pin frequently used QR codes to the Home Screen;
  • A local safety hint on scanned URLs (distinguishing HTTP/HTTPS and warning on suspicious links).

The App is completely free, with no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or ads, and runs offline by default with no account registration required.

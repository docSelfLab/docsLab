PinStamp Privacy Policy

Effective 18 September 2026 · Contact: contactworking@163.com

This policy explains how the iOS app PinStamp (“the App”) handles information. The App does not offer account registration and does not operate a first-party user identity database.

1. What we store

Content you create is stored mainly on your device, including:

  • Map pins / saved places (name, coordinates, address, notes, and similar fields)
  • The currently selected place (used by Map, Weather, Camera, and photo geotag), which may include GPS altitude when available
  • In-app indexes for stamped photos
  • Preferences (for example stamp style, overlay place text when writing GPS)

The App contains no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, and no advertising. Data stays on device by default and is shared via an App Group snapshot so the Home Screen widget can show your latest place. This version does not upload your place list to a first-party cloud backup service.

2. System permissions

  • Location (When In Use): center the map on your current position, read GPS altitude when available, weather for “my location,” and related photo GPS. We do not request Always location and do not track you in the background.
  • Camera: stamped photo capture.
  • Photo Library: read photos you choose for GPS editing or related processing; writes are new copies and do not overwrite originals you did not choose to change. If overlay place text is enabled, that text is burned into the copy’s pixels.

You can revoke these permissions in iOS Settings at any time. Features that need a permission will stop working; others (such as dropping a pin manually) remain available.

3. Weather and maps

Weather is provided by Apple WeatherKit for the coordinate you select. Apple processes those requests under its terms and privacy policy. The App shows Apple’s required weather attribution.

Sharing a place or opening directions may generate Apple Maps or Google Maps links. Opening those links leaves the App and is governed by the other party’s policies.

This version does not send coordinates to third-party elevation services. Altitude for map taps / search is usually unavailable; current GPS may show altitude only when vertical accuracy allows.

4. Purchases

There are none. The App is fully free: it does not offer subscriptions, in-app purchases, or paid unlocks, and it does not integrate any payment SDK.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not change iPhone system location or provide fake GPS to other apps
  • We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in this version
  • We do not send marketing push notifications in this version

6. Deleting data

Settings provides a control to delete data the App stores (places and in-app stamped-photo indexes, among other items). Uninstalling the App also removes App container data on device (subject to iOS backup behavior).

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date on this page. Continued use means you understand the updated policy. Questions: contactworking@163.com.

SmartCleam Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026 · Effective date: September 22, 2026

SmartCleam (the "App") is developed by selfLab ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the App treats your information. Our core promise: all analysis and processing of your photos, videos, contacts and calendar data takes place locally on your device. We do not collect, upload, or disclose your personal content to any third party.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect any personal content data. Specifically:

  • Photos and videos: used only to identify duplicates, similar items, screenshots, large files and related cleanup categories locally on your device. Scan results stay on your device. Photos and videos are never uploaded to any server.
  • Contacts: read only when you open Contacts Cleanup on the Tools tab, for local deduplication, merging and backup export. Contact data is never uploaded.
  • Calendar: read only when you open Calendar Cleanup on the Tools tab, to identify outdated and duplicate events locally. Calendar data is never uploaded.
  • Account information: the App requires no registration or login, and does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers or any other identity information.

2. System Permissions

  • Photos (read): used to scan and clean the photo library. Denying this permission limits cleanup features that need library access.
  • Photos (add): used only when you actively use Video Compression (or a similar export) and choose to save the result back to your photo library.
  • Contacts: used only for Contacts Cleanup on the Tools tab.
  • Calendar: used only for Calendar Cleanup on the Tools tab.

3. Data Storage

Scan results, the temporary pending-delete confirmation list, and cleanup statistics are stored locally on your device (inside the app sandbox and the App Group container used for the home screen widget). Uninstalling the App removes them. We operate no remote database that stores your personal content.

4. Third-Party Services

The App integrates no advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs or any third-party tracking services. Your data is never shared with third parties.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 14 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your Rights

  • You can revoke the App's photo, contacts or calendar access at any time in the system Settings;
  • Every photo library deletion requires your confirmation in a system dialog. The App cannot bypass that confirmation;
  • Uninstalling the App erases all data it has stored locally.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page, and material changes will be announced in the App.

1. What is Cup Water Track?

Cup Water Track is a hydration habit app. It helps you log water intake, set a daily goal, receive local reminders, view basic statistics, and optionally sync water data with Apple Health after authorization.

2. What does Pro include?

Cup Water Track Pro includes unlimited drink logging and the Home Screen widget. Prices, subscription periods, trials, and purchase options are shown in the in-app purchase page and the App Store.

3. Why can I not see Health sync?

  1. Open Cup Water Track -> Settings -> Apple Health, then enable Health sync.
  2. Check iOS Settings to confirm Health permission is enabled for Cup Water Track.
  3. Return to the app and log a new drink.

If Health permission is denied, local hydration tracking still works.

4. Why did I not receive a reminder?

  1. Check iOS Settings -> Notifications -> Cup Water Track.
  2. Check that reminders are enabled in the app settings.
  3. Check whether Focus mode, Low Power Mode, or Notification Summary delayed the notification.

5. Where is my data stored?

The current version primarily stores data on your device. Apple Health sync writes data to Apple Health only after your authorization. The app does not operate its own backend server for hydration logs.

6. How do I restore Pro?

Open Settings -> Upgrade to Pro, then tap Restore Purchase on the subscription page. Use the same Apple ID that was used for the original purchase.

7. What if purchase or restore fails?

  1. Check your network connection.
  2. Confirm that you are signed in to the correct Apple ID.
  3. Restart the app and try Restore Purchase again.

If the issue continues, email contactworking@163.com with your device model, iOS version, app version, time of issue, and screenshots if available.

8. How do I delete my records?

The current version does not provide per-entry deletion in the app. You can uninstall the app to remove local app data. If data has been synced to Apple Health, manage that data in the iOS Health app.

9. Is this a medical app?

No. Cup Water Track is a wellness habit tool. It does not provide medical advice and does not replace professional medical diagnosis or treatment.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 16, 2026
App: Cup Water Track
Contact: contactworking@163.com

Cup Water Track, also known as WaterTrack or 喝水侠 in Chinese locales, values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app processes and how it is used.

1. Information We Process

  • Local app data: hydration logs, drink type, amount, log time, daily goal, reminder settings, onboarding status, and free-trial usage count.
  • HealthKit data: water intake data is read or written only after you grant Apple Health permission.
  • Notification permission status: used for scheduled local hydration reminders, not remote push notifications.
  • In-app purchase status: StoreKit purchase, restore, and Pro entitlement status. Apple handles payment processing.
  • Widget shared data: to support the Home Screen widget, today's hydration summary is shared locally between the main app and the widget extension through Apple App Group.

We do not require account registration. We do not operate our own backend server for hydration logs. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide hydration logging, daily goals, statistics, and reminders.
  • Sync hydration data with Apple Health when authorized.
  • Display today's hydration progress in the Home Screen widget for Pro users.
  • Handle Pro entitlement checks, purchases, and purchase restore.
  • Troubleshoot and improve app reliability.

3. Permissions and System Features

  • HealthKit: used only after your consent. Local tracking still works if permission is denied.
  • Notifications: used for local reminder notifications. Manual logging still works if permission is denied.
  • StoreKit: Apple handles payment processing. We do not store card or bank information.
  • WidgetKit / App Group: used to share a hydration snapshot locally between the app and widget extension. No network transfer is involved.

4. Storage and Security

The current version stores data primarily on your device using SwiftData, UserDefaults, and the App Group shared container. We take reasonable measures to protect data, but no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Third-Party Services

The app uses Apple platform services including Apple HealthKit, Apple StoreKit, Apple UserNotifications, and Apple WidgetKit. We do not sell personal information.

6. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13.

7. Your Choices

  • You can disable HealthKit or notification permissions in iOS Settings.
  • You can remove the Home Screen widget to stop widget display.
  • You can uninstall the app to remove local app data and preferences, subject to iOS behavior.
  • Data already written to Apple Health can be managed in the iOS Health app.

The current version does not provide per-entry deletion in the app. Uninstalling the app is the primary way to remove local app data.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as features or legal requirements change. Material changes will be announced in the app or on our website.

9. Contact Us

For privacy questions, contact us at contactworking@163.com.

10. Medical Disclaimer
